Where to watch the Bundesliga in Japan

ABEMA (free live broadcasts)

ABEMA shows selected matches live for free every matchday, mainly clubs with Japanese players. It is not every match (DAZN's official news says two matches per matchday).

Free

Checked: October 21, 2026 · Source: ABEMA official Bundesliga 26-27 page ("selected matches free live every matchday, mainly clubs with Japanese players"), DAZN official news (posted 2026-10-15)

Visit the official ABEMA site

DAZN

DAZN streams the Bundesliga live, plus the promotion/relegation play-off and the DFL Supercup. Matches outside ABEMA's free picks are on DAZN too (e.g. Matchday 6, Frankfurt vs Köln).

DAZN Standard: ¥4,200/month, or an annual plan at ¥32,000 paid upfront or ¥3,200/month. ABEMA de DAZN (DAZN inside ABEMA): ¥3,800/month, ¥30,000/year paid upfront, or an annual plan paid monthly at ¥3,000/month (¥36,000 over 12 months). All prices include tax, as of October 2026.

Checked: October 21, 2026 · Source: DAZN official page (prices), DAZN official news (match viewing guide posted 2026-10-15), ABEMA official ABEMA de DAZN league list (Bundesliga 2026-27, season start August 28, 2026)

Visit the official DAZN site

Prices are in Japanese yen and are for viewers in Japan. Services and prices can change, so please check the official site before signing up.
